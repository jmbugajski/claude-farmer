They are properties of this soil, this probe depth and this factory calibration; re-derive if a probe moves. The hand-set Ecowitt alert thresholds they replaced are retired. As of 2026-09-19 both probes' bands are flagged unverified — the derivation counts only downward steps between readings, so the integer probe's ±1 dither accumulates into one-directional loss, and the thresholds are being re-derived. Figures scored against them are withheld rather than shown with a caveat.
